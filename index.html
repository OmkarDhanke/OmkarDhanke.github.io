About Me

I turn operational data into
decisions that move fast.

Currently supporting North America sales operations at General Mills India Center, working across trade planning systems, stakeholder reporting workflows, and retail account data for Kroger and Albertsons. My day-to-day is understanding what operational data actually represents before drawing any conclusions from it.

Outside of work, I build self-driven analytics projects using synthetic and public datasets, not to pad a portfolio, but to practice thinking through real business problems end-to-end. That process has involved detecting $18,348 in simulated SaaS revenue leakage, mapping SLA breach risk across 100,000 support tickets, and building a four-page operational monitoring system in Excel. Each one starts with a business question, not a dataset.

Looking beyond September 2026, I'm prioritizing growth over comfort. I want to work on larger business problems, contribute to decisions that actually move something, and keep building toward roles where analytical work creates measurable impact, not just reports. Whether that's FMCG, operations, or business intelligence, the environment matters more to me than the title.
