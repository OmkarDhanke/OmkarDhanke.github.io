About Me

A data analyst who thinks like
a business person first.

I'm Omkar Dhanke currently working as an FSA Trainee at General Mills India Center, supporting North America sales operations for major retail accounts including Kroger and Albertsons.

My approach to data is simple: understand the business context before touching the numbers. I spend time learning what the data represents operationally where it came from, what process generated it, and what questions actually matter before running a single query.

I hold a B.Sc. in Information Technology from the University of Mumbai (CGPA 8.78/10) with hands-on experience developing analytics solutions in SaaS billing forensics, telecom churn analysis, and operational monitoring, combining SQL, Excel, Python, and BI tools to solve operational and business problems.

Right now I'm gaining enterprise-level exposure to trade planning systems, stakeholder reporting workflows, and platforms like NielsenIQ and BusinessObjects building the operational foundation to grow into a stronger analyst over time.
